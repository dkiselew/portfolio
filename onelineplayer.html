Personal project · Open-Source

OneLinePlayer

An embeddable web video/audio player that needs no installation. The product is the restraint: one line of embed code, a player that stays out of the way.

Role
Concept, UI, code, deploy
Stack
Vanilla JS · Grunt · Cloudflare
Live
onelineplayer.com

Why it looks this way

The marketing page is the product. You configure the player, copy one iframe, and leave. Nothing competes with that loop.

  1. Preview is the hero

    The player sits large on the left so you judge the actual embed, not a mock. The swimming still is content, not chrome.

  2. Controls as a kit, not a settings dump

    Toggles for loop, mute, autoplay sit as a visual strip. Checkboxes below map 1:1 to what the embed will show. You see the surface you are about to ship.

  3. One action

    Embed code and a Copy button. No account, no install, no extra page. Vanilla JS and Cloudflare keep the surface as small as the idea.
